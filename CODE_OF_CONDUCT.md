# Code of Conduct

AITracker is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus on the
technical or product issue rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained disruptive behavior are
not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior violates these expectations. To report conduct concerns privately,
contact the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports
will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical.
